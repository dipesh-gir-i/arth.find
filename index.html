Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Answers to common questions.

What is Arth.Find?

A browser extension that shows you the meaning of any word, phrase, or sentence you select based on the exact context you're reading it in without leaving the page.

How is this different from a regular dictionary or Google search?

A dictionary gives you one generic definition for every use of a word. Arth.Find looks at the sentence (and nearby heading) around your selection and generates a meaning that actually fits what you're reading plus you never leave the page to get it.

Who is it for?

Whether you're a student tackling complex papers, a professional conducting research, or a passionate reader saving on expensive textbooks,this is for you. From engineering diagrams and medical journals to classic literature, master any text directly in your browser.

Do I need to sign up?

No account is required. You bring your own AI provider API key (e.g. from Gemini or OpenAI) to power the explanations.

Why do I need my own API key?

This keeps the extension free to use and puts you in control of your own usage and costs, rather than routing everyone's requests through a shared, rate-limited key.

Is my API key safe?

It's encrypted before it's stored and is never shown back to you in the browser.

Which AI providers are supported?

Gemini, OpenAI and NVIDIA NIM.

There is an API key error when I am trying to use the extension

Remove saved provider key and re-enter it.

Can I change how it looks?

8 color themes and several background options are available in settings.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Arth.Find ("Arth.Find", "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information Arth.Find processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, and the choices available to you when using the Arth.Find Chrome Extension and related services.

By using Arth.Find, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Arth.Find

Arth.Find is a context-aware reading tool that helps users understand words, phrases, and sentences based on the surrounding text in a document.

The service is designed around data minimization. We aim to process only the information necessary to provide the requested explanation and to avoid collecting information that is not needed for the service.

2. Information We Process

Text You Select

When you select a word, phrase, or sentence using Arth.Find, the selected text and relevant surrounding context may be sent to our server so that an explanation can be generated.

We do not require access to your entire document in order to provide an explanation. The service is designed to process the selected text together with the context necessary to understand its meaning.

Information You Provide for AI Services

Arth.Find may allow you to connect your own AI provider account or API key, including supported providers such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, or NVIDIA NIM.

If you choose to use this feature, the credentials you provide are transmitted securely and protected using encryption. Arth.Find does not display your original credential back to you after it has been securely stored.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your AI provider account and for complying with that provider's terms and policies.

Preferences and Settings

Arth.Find may store settings such as reading themes, background preferences, and other interface preferences locally on your device.

These settings are used to provide a more personalized reading experience.

Account Information

Arth.Find does not currently require users to create an account to use the extension.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Arth.Find is used to:

  • provide context-aware explanations and related functionality;
  • securely handle AI provider credentials when you choose to use your own provider account;
  • remember your local preferences and settings;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service where necessary; and
  • comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not use your selected text to build a personal profile about you.

4. AI Providers and Third-Party Services

To generate explanations, Arth.Find may transmit selected text and relevant context to an AI provider.

Depending on how you use the service, this may include providers such as:

  • Google Gemini
  • OpenAI
  • NVIDIA NIM

The information sent to an AI provider is subject to that provider's applicable privacy policy, terms of service, and data-handling practices.

Different providers, products, account types, and service plans may have different data-retention and model-improvement policies. You should review the privacy and data-use terms of the AI provider associated with your account before submitting confidential or sensitive information.

5. Information We Do Not Sell

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

We do not use information processed through Arth.Find to create advertising profiles or credit profiles.

6. Data Retention

Arth.Find is designed to minimize data retention.

Text submitted for an explanation is processed for the purpose of generating the requested result and is not intended to become part of a permanent user history.

We do not maintain a personal reading-history profile associated with an Arth.Find account because an account is not currently required.

Information may nevertheless be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary to operate, protect, troubleshoot, or legally maintain the service.

Where you use your own AI provider credentials, encrypted credential information may remain associated with the service until it is removed, invalidated, or otherwise deleted in accordance with our applicable procedures.

7. Local Storage and Device Data

Some Arth.Find preferences are stored locally on your device, such as themes, wallpapers, and interface settings.

Local settings remain on your device unless you remove them or otherwise clear the relevant browser or extension data.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Arth.Find.

Communications between the extension, our servers, and supported services are protected using encrypted HTTPS connections.

Where you choose to provide an AI provider credential, we use encryption and appropriate security measures designed to protect that credential from unauthorized access.

However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid submitting confidential, highly sensitive, or unnecessary personal information when using AI-powered features.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights
