Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 02, 2026

Arth.Find ("Arth.Find", "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information Arth.Find processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, and the choices available to you when using the Arth.Find Chrome Extension and related services.

By using Arth.Find, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Arth.Find

Arth.Find is a context-aware reading tool that helps users understand words, phrases, and sentences based on the surrounding text in a document. The service is designed around data minimization. We aim to process only the information necessary to provide the requested explanation and to avoid collecting information that is not needed for the service.

2. Information We Process

Text You Select

Selected text and its surrounding context are sent to our server only when you select text and click Explain. Arth.Find does not collect your browsing history and does not read or transmit the rest of a page's content.

Information You Provide for AI Services

Arth.Find may allow you to connect your own AI provider account or API key, including supported providers such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, or NVIDIA NIM. If you choose to use this feature, the credentials you provide are transmitted securely and protected using encryption. Arth.Find does not display your original credential back to you after it has been securely stored. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your AI provider account and for complying with that provider's terms and policies.

Preferences and Settings

Arth.Find may store settings such as reading themes, background preferences, and other interface preferences locally on your device. These settings are used to provide a more personalized reading experience.

Account Information

Arth.Find does not currently require users to create an account to use the extension.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Arth.Find is used to:

  • provide context-aware explanations and related functionality;
  • securely handle AI provider credentials when you choose to use your own provider account;
  • remember your local preferences and settings;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service where necessary; and
  • comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not use your selected text to build a personal profile about you.

4. AI Providers and Third-Party Services

To generate explanations, Arth.Find may transmit selected text and relevant context to an AI provider.

Depending on how you use the service, this may include providers such as:

  • Google Gemini
  • OpenAI
  • NVIDIA NIM

The information sent to an AI provider is subject to that provider's applicable privacy policy, terms of service, and data-handling practices.

Different providers, products, account types, and service plans may have different data-retention and model-improvement policies. You should review the privacy and data-use terms of the AI provider associated with your account before submitting confidential or sensitive information.

5. Information We Do Not Sell

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not use information processed through Arth.Find to create advertising profiles or credit profiles.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received from Chrome APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use this information only to provide Arth.Find's single purpose: explaining selected text in context. We do not sell it or use it for advertising, and we do not transfer it to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We do not store your selected text or its surrounding context on our servers. Text is stored only if you choose to save it to your library, and saved items are kept on your own device. Our servers keep only standard technical logs (such as request time and status) needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Information may nevertheless be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary to operate, protect, troubleshoot, or legally maintain the service. Where you use your own AI provider credentials, encrypted credential information may remain associated with the service until it is removed, invalidated, or otherwise deleted in accordance with our applicable procedures.

7. Local Storage and Device Data

Some Arth.Find preferences are stored locally on your device, such as themes, wallpapers, and interface settings. Local settings remain on your device unless you remove them or otherwise clear the relevant browser or extension data.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Arth.Find. Communications between the extension, our servers, and supported services are protected using encrypted HTTPS connections.

Where you choose to provide an AI provider credential, we use encryption and appropriate security measures designed to protect that credential from unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid submitting confidential, highly sensitive, or unnecessary personal information when using AI-powered features.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may choose whether to use features that require an AI provider credential. You may also remove your locally stored Arth.Find preferences and settings through your browser or extension controls.

Where applicable under the laws governing your use of Arth.Find, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other forms of control over your information. For privacy-related requests or complaints, please contact us using the support details provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Arth.Find is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating user accounts or profiles. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us and you would like it reviewed or removed, please contact us.

11. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements. For users in India, this includes consideration of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules and regulations as they come into force. Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to limit any privacy rights you may have under applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Arth.Find, our services, or applicable legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically to remain informed about how Arth.Find handles information.

We will make reasonable efforts to review and respond to privacy-related requests in a timely manner.
